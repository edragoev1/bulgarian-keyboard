Bulgarian Phonetic Keyboard

Type Bulgarian on a US keyboard: traditional phonetic, with В on V and Ж on W. Then copy the text and paste it where you need it.

This keyboard is made for computers: you type on your own keyboard and get Bulgarian. On a phone, your phone's own Bulgarian keyboard is the better choice; you can add it in the phone's keyboard settings.
